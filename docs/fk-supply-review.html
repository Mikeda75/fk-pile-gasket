What it does

A zip-on, drawstring-cinched sleeve that seals the bottom of a marine piling during concrete or epoxy repair, preventing fill material from spilling into the waterway. It's an environmental-containment product that also saves material and diver bottom time.

Product

Pile Gasket — a zip-on, drawstring-cinched sealing sleeve that closes off the bottom of a marine piling during concrete or epoxy repair, preventing spill of fill material into the waterway and reducing wasted material and diver bottom time.

Also known as: piling jacket seal, bottom bag, pile jacket gasket, piling sealing sleeve.

Application

  • Seals the base of pile-encapsulation forms during underwater concrete/epoxy placement
  • Diver-installed: drawstrings cinch the sleeve to the pile; the vertical zipper closes it around the pile without threading over the top
  • Pile types: timber, steel, concrete CONFIRM — which has it actually been used on?
  • Fill compatibility: works with whatever concrete the job specifies — no special mix requirements
  • Removed after the fill cures — not a permanent or sacrificial form
