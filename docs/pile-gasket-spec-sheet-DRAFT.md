## Product

**Pile Gasket** — a zip-on, drawstring-cinched sealing sleeve that closes off the bottom of a marine piling during concrete or epoxy repair, preventing spill of fill material into the waterway and reducing wasted material and diver bottom time.

Also known as: piling jacket seal, bottom bag, pile jacket gasket.

## Application

- Seals the base of pile-encapsulation forms ("pile jackets") during underwater concrete/epoxy placement
- Installed by divers; drawstrings at top and bottom cinch the sleeve to the pile, vertical zipper closes it around the pile without threading over the top
- Suitable for timber, steel, and concrete piles **[CONFIRM — which pile types has it actually been used on?]**
- Fill compatibility: works with whatever concrete the job specifies for the pile repair — no special mix requirements
- **Removed after the fill cures** — not a permanent or sacrificial form
