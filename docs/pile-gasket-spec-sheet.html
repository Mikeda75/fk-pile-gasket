PILE GASKET

Zippered fabric bottom-seal gasket for pile encapsulation forms
Also known as: pile jacket bottom seal · piling jacket seal · bottom bag · grout containment gasket
Description

The FK Pile Gasket is a made-to-order waterproof fabric gasket that seals the base of a pile encapsulation form ("pile jacket") during underwater concrete placement — keeping fill material in the form and out of the waterway. It replaces improvised bottom seals (stuffed foam, pleated plastic "diaper" skirts, banding, and bolted clamps) with a one-piece unit a diver zips around the pile and cinches tight. Designed by a working commercial diver who installs pile jackets for a living.

Features
  • One-piece bottom seal — zip on, cinch two drawstrings, done
  • Reduces installation time and diver bottom time
  • Contains grout/concrete at the form base — reduces wasted fill and waterway spill
  • Full-length Lenzip® #10 locking zipper with stops — installs around the pile, no threading over the top
  • Dual drawstring cinch channels (top and bottom hems) — 1/4" solid braid nylon cords
  • Non-corrosive, lightweight, waterproof PVC-backed fabric
  • For timber, steel, and concrete piles; round or square profiles
  • Made to order in the USA (Bayville, NJ) — built to your pile dimensions

Application DRAFT
  1. Verify size. Confirm gasket circumference and height against the pile and the jacket base elevation before the dive.
  2. Prepare the pile. At the gasket elevation, remove marine growth and loose material per project specifications.
  3. Wrap and zip. Open the gasket, wrap it around the pile at the base of the encapsulation form, and close the full-length zipper. The locking slider and zipper stops hold it closed under load.
  4. Cinch the bottom. Draw the bottom drawstring tight against the pile and tie off.
  5. Seal to the form. Cinch the top drawstring over the base of the pile jacket and tie off, closing the jacket-to-pile transition.
  6. Fill the form. Place concrete/grout per project specification at a controlled rate, hose extended to the lowest point of the form. Do not free-drop fill through water.

Installation sequence shown is typical; conform to project drawings and specifications. Steps 3–5 are performed by the dive crew.
