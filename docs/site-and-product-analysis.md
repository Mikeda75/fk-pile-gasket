  - **Two drawstrings** — one at top, one at bottom — for cinching to the pile
  - **High-tensile-strength thread** stitching

## Competitive landscape (researched 2026-07-27)

Nobody sells a purpose-made, standalone fabric bottom-seal gasket at a published price. What contractors use today:

| Product / method | Who | What it is | Weakness vs Pile Gasket |
|---|---|---|---|
| SeaShield Fab-Form | Denso | Nylon ballistic-fabric pile jacket with zipper closure, custom-fabricated | It's the whole encasement form, not the bottom seal; quote-only, big-company sales process |
| Skirt Pins + plastic "diaper" | PileMedic | Heavy plastic sheeting, 6+ pins, zip-tie bands, foam stuffing | Improvised multi-part assembly, single-use, fiddly diver work |
| ShearFlange / friction clamp | PileMedic | Bolted metal half-rings supporting the jacket | Hardware to bolt underwater; still needs foam for the seal |
| Foam bottom seals | PileJax | Single-use profiled foam wrap, or metal clamp + foam gasket | System-specific accessory, not sold standalone |
| FX-70-6FS epoxy bottom seal | Simpson Strong-Tie | Fast-set underwater epoxy poured as a 6–12" seal plug | Chemical approach; cure time, cost, mixing on site |
| Generic practice (per MnDOT/FRP studies) | — | Foam stuffed between jacket and pile, pleated plastic + nylon banding | Pure improvisation — exactly what the Pile Gasket replaces |

**Positioning takeaways:**
- Kevin's niche is real: a **purpose-built, one-piece, diver-friendly bottom seal** (zip on, cinch two drawstrings, done) vs. improvised foam-and-plastic diapers or bolted hardware.
- **Published pricing is a differentiator** — every competitor is quote-only. A typical unit (51" × 36" ≈ 12.75 → 13 sq ft) is ~$79. That's cheap enough to put the price on the site proudly.
- Useful search terms buyers use: pile jacket bottom seal, grout containment, pile encapsulation, fabric form. The new site's copy should use this industry vocabulary.
- "Reduces diver bottom time" matches the industry's own pain points (multi-part seals are called out as fiddly diver work in the literature).
